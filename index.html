about us

It started with a jar on the windowsill.

Saturday Bakehouse began the way most good habits do: by accident. A jar of flour and water got left on a windowsill, started to bubble, and was given a name. Doris needed feeding, so we baked. Then we baked too much, so we gave it to the neighbours. Then the neighbours started asking for more.

We still bake in a small kitchen with one oven and a lot of patience. Our loaves rest for up to 36 hours before they see heat. Our croissants take three days. We can’t make a lot, and we’ve stopped apologising for that — it’s what keeps everything honest.

So we bake once a week, on Saturday, for the people who asked. No shelf, no leftovers, nothing sitting under a lamp. Just bread and pastry made for you, by name, and handed over still warm.
